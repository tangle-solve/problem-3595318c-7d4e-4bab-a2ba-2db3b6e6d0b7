### CLIM-000228: Mixed-phase cloud amount versus phase feedback
- **Subfield:** Radiative forcing, clouds, and climate sensitivity
- **Open:** Establish the combined warming response of supercooled-liquid fraction, cloud coverage, and precipitation. Parameterizations must match cloud-top and interior observations without compensating cloud-amount and optical-depth errors.
- **Status:** A 2016 single-model constraint increased sensitivity; a 2025 multi-model analysis found opposing feedbacks that substantially offset. The cancellation’s robustness remains uncertain.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Controls polar and extratropical feedbacks and model sensitivity.
- **Key works:** Tan, Storelvmo and Zelinka (2016), [“Observational constraints on mixed-phase clouds imply higher climate sensitivity”](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aad5300); Tan et al. (2025), [“Moderate climate sensitivity due to opposing mixed-phase cloud feedbacks”](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41612-025-00948-7).
- **Related:** Heterogeneous ice nucleation; multiphase turbulent transport.
